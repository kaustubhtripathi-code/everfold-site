The EU General Product Safety Regulation (2023/988) has been fully applicable since 13 December 2024. Marketplaces now ask for Declaration of Conformity and Responsible Person data listing by listing, and delist the ones that cannot produce it. Passfile keeps that evidence per SKU, versioned, for the full ten-year retention period.

Ninety seconds on how it works

A delisting is a paperwork failure, not a product failure.

Your product is safe and your test reports exist. They are in an email thread, a supplier's Drive folder, and a PDF someone renamed. When a marketplace asks for the file behind a specific listing, the answer has to arrive in hours and it has to match the SKU on the label.

Small brands lose listings on that gap alone. Passfile closes it by keeping the record where the SKU is, not where the email was.

Four steps, one permanent record

01 · Vault

The technical file

A private vault per SKU: test reports, risk assessments, supplier declarations, artwork and labels, standards applied. One place, one product, ten-year retention built in.

02 · Declare

Declaration of Conformity

Passfile generates the DoC from what is actually in the vault and cites each supporting document by its SHA-256 fingerprint. The declaration points at specific files, not at filenames that can quietly change.

03 · Publish

Public record + QR

A permanent public page for the SKU, with a QR code you can put on the packaging. Marketplaces, retailers and authorities read the record; the underlying documents stay private.

04 · Version

Every change kept

Update a report or a standard and the record gains a new version. Older versions stay reachable, so a unit shipped two years ago still resolves to the record it was sold under.

It tells you before the marketplace does

  • 01Gap alerts — a SKU missing a document its declaration needs is flagged as incomplete rather than sitting quietly as done.
  • 02Expiry alerts — test reports and certificates that have run out are surfaced ahead of the date, not after a request arrives.
  • 03Superseded-standard alerts — when a harmonised standard your declaration cites is replaced, the affected SKUs are listed for you.
  • 04Fingerprinted evidence — every file carries a SHA-256 digest, so a document swapped after the fact is visible instead of invisible.

What Passfile is not

Passfile is record-keeping software. It is not your Responsible Person under GPSR, it does not certify or test products, and it does not give legal advice. Responsibility for the accuracy of a declaration, and for appointing an EU or UK Responsible Person, stays with the economic operator placing the product on the market.
