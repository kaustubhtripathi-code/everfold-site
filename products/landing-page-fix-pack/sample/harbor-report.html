01 / Prioritized findings

High priority

1. Name the customer in the first screen

Location: Hero headline

Evidence: Source hero reads “More time for what matters.”

Observation: The headline does not identify bookkeeping or the intended customer.

Reasoning / hypothesis: A new visitor may need to read further to establish relevance. This is a hypothesis, not measured behavior.

Recommended fix: Lead with bookkeeping for independent design studios.

High priority

2. Explain what the button starts

Location: Hero button

Evidence: Primary button reads “Get started”.

Observation: The button does not describe the next step.

Reasoning / hypothesis: A more specific label may reduce uncertainty about the commitment.

Recommended fix: Use “Request an introductory call” and explain that availability will be confirmed by email.

Medium priority

3. Make the monthly scope concrete

Location: Services section

Evidence: Section says “We handle the details. You focus on the big picture.”

Observation: The section does not name any deliverables.

Reasoning / hypothesis: Buyers need a scope they can assess against their needs.

Recommended fix: List transaction categorization, reconciliation and monthly summary, only if the owner confirms these are included.

Headline

CURRENT More time for what matters.
